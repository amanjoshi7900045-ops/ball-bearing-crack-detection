This is a separate concept from the microwave version. The bearing is inspected without physical probe contact using an air gap between the ultrasonic transducer pair and the metal surface.

Concept simulation only: displayed ultrasonic values are not live transducer measurements and do not prove crack-detection accuracy.

Important engineering constraint: air-coupled ultrasound has much weaker coupling from air into metal than contact ultrasonic testing. The physical prototype will require suitable high-sensitivity air-coupled transducers, careful alignment, amplification and signal processing.

Scanning strategy

  • Rotate the ball continuously.
  • Move/tilt the ball so previously supported regions become exposed.
  • Keep a known air stand-off between the transducers and ball.
  • Use overlapping scan passes instead of a single pass.
  • Record ball orientation and sensor position with every measurement.

Concept dimensions

  • Ball diameter: 20 mm
  • Illustrative air stand-off: 10 mm
  • Bottom-mounted non-contact TX/RX head
  • XYZ positioning carriage
  • Three repositionable support rollers
